# Project Serafina glossary

- **Report**: One supplied CoStar document, including every page, appendix, table, chart, and note.
- **Report revision**: One retained extraction of a Report with its source identity, extraction settings, and evidence coverage.
- **Evidence**: Source text, table cells, page coordinates, or the original page supporting a claim. Evidence is distinct from interpretation.
- **Coverage**: An account of which Report pages were read and structurally processed, including failures and unreadable pages.
- **Fact**: An interpreted value qualified by entity, metric, period, scope, and supporting Evidence. Unknown and conflicting values are not zero.
- **Subject Property**: The property described by the Report; comparable properties and market aggregates are separate entities.
- **Score eligibility**: Whether all enabled score factors have valid inputs. Missing evidence cannot establish a negative investment decision.
- **Workbook draft**: A populated underwriting workbook with remaining inputs and calculation status carried inside the artifact.
